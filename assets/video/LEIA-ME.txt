Optional hero background video.

Drop a file named  hero.mp4  in this folder:
  - 10-20 seconds, horizontal, 1080p
  - NO audio, seamless loop
  - keep it light (~2-4 MB)

It layers automatically over the animated background in the hero section
(the <video> element is already wired in index.html). Without the file,
nothing breaks - the hero uses the CSS animated backdrop + hero-bg.svg.

For a YouTube/Vimeo clip in the lightbox instead, point any element with
  data-video-dialog  data-video-src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/XXXX"
